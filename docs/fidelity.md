Known gaps (docs/models.md, simplifications column, truncated):

- **BLDC / DC motor + thermal**: Line-to-line `r20` used as the winding resistance of a DC-equivalent motor; no cogging, no saturation; iron / bearing / windage loss only through the lumped `tau_f` above (explicit torque: with `v > 0` also keep `dt v Kt i0 / (w0 J) < 2`). `w_s` is a smoothing speed (stability: `dt < 2 J w_s / (Kt i0)`).
