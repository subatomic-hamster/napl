# Dog actuator pre-study (SP3a, analytic)

Generated by `python3 tools/dog_actuator_study.py` (stdlib only; tables below are its output of 2026-10-16, prose is ours). It shortlists
candidates for the SP3b full-sim sweep; it does not replace it. All part numbers come from `parts/` (prices dated in each part file).

## Model (one paragraph, see the script docstring)

Planar leg, thigh = shank = L, foot under the hip at a half-knee angle of 40 degrees. Actuator: linear torque-speed line
tau(w) = tau_peak (1 - w/w0), w0 = KV_output x V (voltage-limited), tau_peak current-limited. Knee drive ratio r multiplies knee torque
and divides knee speed. Static peak foot force F = r tau_peak / (L sin 40 deg). Trot at duty 0.5 with 25 degrees hip sweep: top speed
is min(swing bound = stride-frequency bound, stance bound = torque bound), the actuator-limited gait reasoning of Katz (MIT Mini Cheetah
actuator, 2018) and Kim/Katz et al. (Mini Cheetah, ICRA 2019). Stance peak force = 1.5 (dynamic margin, assumed) x 1.57 (sinusoidal profile)
x m g / 2 on two legs. Mass and cost add the fixed parts (table below). Not modelled: gait inertia, knee-ratio loss, thermal limits,
flying trot (duty < 0.5), terrain. These are what SP3b measures.

## Findings against the Review Focus

1. **Torque/speed side labelling:** every torque, speed and current of the new actuator files is labelled output or motor side in the file
   header; `tests/python/test_dog_parts.py::test_qdd_constants_reproduce_datasheet` reproduces peak torque and no-load speed within 10 %
   from (Kt motor, N, eta, Ke). Caveat: `efficiency` is an effective figure derived from the published peak torque and peak current (it
   absorbs the supply-side-versus-phase current definition), so the torque half of that test is a consistency check, bounded to 0.6-0.95;
   the speed half is independent (V / (Ke N) against the published no-load speed).
2. **Battery margin (Review Focus 3): not met by the 70 A pack.** The estimated simultaneous stance peak is **73-87 A** with the GIM8108-8
   (86 A for AK70-9) against the Auline 8S1P pack's 70 A continuous rating (margin 0.8-1.0x) and the Daly BMS's 60 A (0.7-0.8x). It is a
   stall-current-scaled worst case (current proportional to torque, 1.5 dynamic margin), probably pessimistic, but it says: use the knee drive
   ratio 1.5-2 (cuts the peak to 73-78 A), a software `i_limit` below the stall current, and plan an 8S2P pack or a 100 A BMS for the build.
   Energy: 133 Wh; at 250 W average that is 32 minutes.
3. **Speed:** the torque-limited trot bound is 2.4 m/s (GIM8108-8) to 2.8 m/s (AK70-9) at 33.6 V, below the 3.5 m/s criterion of the spec.
   The swing bound alone is 4.9 m/s, so the gap is the stance torque at the hip; reaching 3.5 m/s needs the flying-trot / bounding gaits
   (lower duty, smaller stance torque per unit time) that SP3b simulates. Say so honestly in the README if it stays below.
4. **Cost:** the 12 actuators plus the fixed parts give 5.1 k USD with the GIM8108-8 (7.3 k with AK70-9), over the 3-4.5 k target. The GIM6010-8
   would put the dog near 4.1 k USD but is torque-infeasible in this model. 47 % of the fixed 2.54 k USD is price-estimated (Mid-360 at the
   vendor's own 749 USD rather than the 1,092 USD a reseller listed, BMS, PDB, the printed parts, the antenna). Prices drift: all are dated
   2026-10-16 in the part files; two of them (D435i, Jetson) are listed out of stock or at a marketplace price.
5. **Lidar head geometry (Review Focus 4):** the Mid-360 sees -7 to +52 degrees; its blind zone is a 0.1 m sphere and the *ground* blind
   cone is set by the lower FOV edge. Sensor 0.45 m above ground, front foot 0.30 m ahead of it (both estimates):

   No tilt makes the Mid-360 see the ground *under* the front feet without losing the rear sky. The head mount is printed at 25 degrees
   (`DOG_LIDAR_TILT` regenerates it): foot placement relies on the accumulated height map for the last 0.4 m, which at 1.5 m/s is a 0.3 s old
   map. 35 degrees is the alternative if the mapping needs more near-field and can lose the upper 35 degrees of the cone.

## Candidates for the SP3b sweep

1. **GIM8108-8, r = 1.5, L = 0.22, 33.6 V** (primary): 22 N*m class, the only low-cost actuator that passes the torque check with margin;
   peak foot force 257 N (5.6x body weight on two legs); 5.1 k USD.
2. **GIM8108-8, r = 2.0, L = 0.20, 33.6 V**: the high-force point (peak foot force 342 N); lowest bus peak (73 A); sweep L = 0.20/0.22/0.25 around it.
3. **GIM6010-8, r = 2.0, L = 0.20, 33.6 V** (budget probe, ~4.1 k USD): infeasible under the 1.5 dynamic margin, feasible without it; the sweep
   should find whether a lighter dog (< 9 kg) and gaits below 2 m/s still meet the rocky-field criterion at 1.5 m/s. AK70-9 (7.3 k USD) is a
   comparison only: it is faster (2.8 m/s) but 40 % over the money.
