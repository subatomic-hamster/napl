# SO-101 follower arm: parts research (stage N1c)

Accessed 2026-10-06. Parts files: `parts/arm/*.toml` (17 files). Meshes: `meshes/so101/` (13 STL, 16 MB).
Everything below is the follower arm only; the leader arm (different gear ratios, handle, trigger) is out of scope.

Servo variant per joint (follower): all six joints = STS3215 C001, 7.4 V, 1/345.
- README: "The STS3215 motors for the follower arm comes in two sizes. The 7.4V has a stall torque of 16.5kg.cm at 6V (and likely slightly less for a 5V power supply). The 12V version has a stall torque of 30kg.cm. ... Note if you do this, you will also have to buy a 12V 5A+ power supply instead of a 5V one. The leader arm is always 7.4V for the SO101."
- LeRobot docs (https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/so101): "The follower arm uses 6x STS3215 motors with 1/345 gearing."
- Seeed wiki spec table (https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/lerobot_so100m/): follower "ST-3215-C001(7.4V) / C018(12V) / C047(12V) 1:345 F1-F6"; Standard kit PSU "5.5 mm × 2.1 mm DC 5 V 4 A", Pro kit "12 V 2 A".
- Leader (not modelled): joint 1 and 3 C044 1/191, joint 2 C001 1/345, joints 4-6 C046 1/147.
- 12V variant (C018/C047, optional upgrade, no parts file written, "one per variant used"): Waveshare ST3215 page lists "Input Voltage: 6-12.6V", "No load Speed: 0.222sec/60° (45RPM)@12V", "No Load Current: 200 mA", "Locked-rotor Current: 2.7 A", "High torque, up to 30kg.cm@12V" (https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ST3215_Servo). Add `feetech-sts3215-c018` if the 12V arm is wanted.

Important consequence: the default follower runs 7.4 V-class servos from a **5 V** supply, below the 6 V datasheet column. Our model predicts 1.32 N*m stall and 35 RPM no-load at 5 V (derivations in the servo file).

Sum 0.632 kg minus 6 x 0.055 kg servo = 0.302 kg for printed parts + screws + horns, which is 43 % of the 0.708 kg a fully solid PLA (1.24 g/cm^3) print of the 571 cm^3 would weigh, consistent with 15 % infill plus shells. The URDF does not say what it includes (screws, cables, driver board are probably not).

MJCF servo defaults (`so101_new_calib.xml`, class `sts3215`): `joint damping="0.60" frictionloss="0.052" armature="0.028"`, `position kp="998.22" kv="2.731" forcerange="-2.94 2.94"` with the comment "These position gains and forces were calculated according to https://github.com/Gregory119/RBE501-RL-arm-project/blob/main/gymnasium_env/README.md, assuming that the servo proportional gain is set to 16." `joints_properties.xml` at the same commit differs (`kp="17.8" kv="0.0" forcerange="-3.35 3.35"`). Both force ranges (2.94 N*m = 30 kg.cm, 3.35 N*m) exceed the 7.4 V servo stall torque (1.91 N*m at 7.4 V); the 2.94 value equals the **12V** variant's 30 kg.cm. Do not take the MJCF actuator limits as the 7.4 V servo's. A `backlash` class (+/-0.5 deg = 0.008727 rad, damping 0.01, armature 0.01) is defined but not applied to any joint.

## STS3215 servo (C001) key data

Source S1: Feetech "STS3215 product specification" A/0, 2020-04-10, machine-translated copy at https://core-electronics.com.au/attachments/uploads/sts3215-smart-servo-datasheet-translated.pdf. The PDF is image-based (no text layer): a verifier must view pages 3-4, 6, 8. Titled "7.4V 19KG.CM plastic shell metal tooth magnetic coding dual-axis TTL serial servo".

| Item | 6 V | 7.4 V |
|---|---|---|
| No-load speed | 0.238 s/60 deg (42 RPM) | 0.192 s/60 deg (52 RPM) |
| No-load current | 130 mA | 150 mA |
| Stall torque | 16.5 kg.cm (1.618 N*m) | 19.5 kg.cm (1.912 N*m) |
| Stall current | 2 A | 2.5 A |
| Rated torque / current | 4 kg.cm / 500 mA | 5 kg.cm / 650 mA |

Other: idle 6 mA; working voltage 4-7.4 V; terminal resistance 2.5 ohm; Kt "8kg.cm/A" (output side); size 45.2 x 24.7 x 35 mm; mass 55 +/- 1 g; gear ratio 1/345; backlash <= 0.5 deg; 12-bit magnetic encoder, 0.088 deg/count, 360 deg (4096 counts); half-duplex async serial, 38400 bps to 1 Mbps (factory 1 Mbps), ID 0-253; max position update rate 1 ms; modes 0 angle servo, 1 closed-loop speed, 2 open-loop speed (PWM), 3 step; protection: overload "after being blocked at more than 80% of the locked rotor and lasts 2s", over-current ">2A and lasts for 2s", over-temp 70 C; life ">100000 times". Gear material: openelab C044 page "Copper gears" (same family, metal train); case PA+GF. Output horn 25T, M3x6 horn screw, 8 x PA2.0 self-tapping mounting screws.

Memory table (third-party transcription of the Feetech manual, https://aifitlab-wiki.super.site/feetech-servo-motor-docs/feetech-sts-servo-memory-table-analysis; LeRobot's `tables.py` confirms addresses, official manual link http://doc.feetech.cn/#/prodinfodownload?srcType=FT-SMS-STS-emanual-229f4476422d4059abfb1cb0 was not retrievable): P gain addr 21 (0x15), D gain addr 22 (0x16), I gain addr 23 (0x17), each 0-254; Kp "scaled by 1/4", Kd "scaled by 1/8" per that transcription. Max torque addr 16 (default 1000, 0.1 %), torque limit addr 48 (default 1000), protection current addr 28 (default 511 x 6.5 mA), overload torque addr 36 (80 %), protection time addr 35 (200 x 10 ms), holding torque after overload addr 34 (20 %), overcurrent time addr 38 (200 x 10 ms), dead zones addr 26/27 (default 1, 0.087 deg), acceleration addr 41 (8.7 deg/s^2 unit), max temp addr 13 (70 C), present current addr 69 (6.5 mA unit). ### Derived DC-motor equivalent (see derivations in `parts/arm/feetech-sts3215-c001.toml`)

- R_eff = 2.98 ohm: stall V/I = 6/2.0 = 3.00 and 7.4/2.5 = 2.96 ohm; datasheet terminal R is 2.5 ohm, so ~0.48 ohm is driver + wiring.
- Ke = Kt = 3.695e-3 V*s/rad from no-load points: (7.4 - 0.15*2.98)/(345*5.4542 rad/s) = 3.695e-3; at 6 V gives 3.697e-3 (agreement 0.06 %).
- Gear efficiency eta = 0.658 (stall: 0.638 at 7.4 V, 0.678 at 6 V); independent cross-check from the datasheet Kt 8 kg.cm/A gives 0.615.
- No-load friction at the motor shaft Kt*I0 = 0.554 mN*m.
- Rotor inertia 2.35e-7 kg*m^2 = estimated (MJCF armature 0.028 / 345^2).
- Model reproduces datasheet stall torque at 6 V to -1.9 % and at 7.4 V to +2.4 % (this is partly fitting, since eta is fitted to those points; the independent checks are no-load speed at 6 V and 7.4 V, and 5 V predictions). Predicted rated-point current at 5 kg.cm is 0.73 A vs 0.65 A published (+13 %).
- 5 V prediction (actual BOM supply): stall 1.32 N*m (13.5 kg.cm), no-load 3.69 rad/s (35 RPM). README says only "likely slightly less" than the 16.5 kg.cm at 6 V; no published 5 V figure.

## Other electronics

- Waveshare Bus Servo Adapter (A): 16 g ("weight" value 16 unit g in the product page structured data), 42.00 x 33.00 mm, mounting holes 2.50 mm at 37.00 x 28.00 mm spacing, "Power Supply DC 9~12.6V (ST series servo)", DC 5.5 x 2.1 mm, UART/USB-C via jumper A/B, up to 253 servos. Note the mismatch: the board's rated input is 9-12.6 V while the SO-101 BOM feeds it 5 V for 7.4 V servos. No current rating published (placeholder estimated 5 A). Source: https://www.waveshare.com/bus-servo-adapter-a.htm and https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/Bus_Servo_Adapter_(A).
- PSU: Amazon title "Facmogu 5V 4A AC to DC Power Supply Adapter, AC 100-240V to DC 5 Volt 4 Amp ..." with 5.5x2.5 and 5.5x2.1 mm barrel tips. Mass, output impedance, current limit are estimated. The WebFetch result for this page covered only the first 100 kB of a 1.5 MB page; the title was the only spec text read.
- Cable: datasheet 6-7 "Type 5264-3P, Material PP, line length 15CM", pins GND / Vcc / Signal. Gauge not published; 26 AWG estimated, resistance 0.1339 ohm/m per conductor from the AWG table (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_wire_gauge). Servo power is daisy-chained, so segment resistances add up toward the base.

## Validation targets for N6

1. Servo bench: stall torque 1.912 N*m at 7.4 V and 1.618 N*m at 6 V; no-load 5.454 rad/s (7.4 V) and 4.400 rad/s (6 V); stall current 2.5 A / 2.0 A; no-load current 0.15 / 0.13 A; within +/-10 % per the plan.
2. Arm mass: 0.632 kg (sum of URDF link masses; CAD estimate). Third-party claim (not verified, inconsistent elsewhere on that site: it also says "15 kg·cm stall torque", "12V DC 3A"): "Weight (arm only): ~800 g", "Payload: ~500 g Varies with arm pose and servo temperature", "Reach: ~500 mm" (https://roboticscenter.ai/hardware/so-101/specs). A search-result summary also attributes "Payload: 500 g" to the Seeed SO-ARM101 **Pro** kit (12 V servos); not fetched or verified. The repo, LeRobot docs and Seeed wiki publish no payload or arm-mass figure.
3. Reach: no published figure in the repo; compute from the URDF kinematics at build time and compare with the unverified third-party "~500 mm".
4. Position resolution 0.088 deg (4096 counts), backlash <= 0.5 deg, dead zone default 1 count.

## Gaps (estimated, flagged in the parts files)

- Printed-part perimeter/wall count (est. 2); per-part printed mass (none published; computed later from volume x PLA density x infill/shell model).
- D-gain factory default (est. 32); Kp/Kd/Ki internal control law scaling beyond the transcription's "scaled by 1/4" and "1/8" is unverified.
- Rotor inertia (est. from MJCF armature) and any viscous/Coulomb split of the loss.

- `parts/README.md` shows multi-line inline tables in its example, which Python tomllib (TOML 1.0) rejects; all arm files use single-line inline tables. The drone parts currently in the tree fail the checker for exactly this reason (not touched).
