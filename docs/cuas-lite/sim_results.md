# cuas-lite sim results (Monte Carlo, lockstep)

Generated by `python examples/cuas-lite/mc.py --runs 30 --kinds balloon drift hanging quad3 quad8 --bands 3-5 5-10 10-15 --person-runs 30 --seed0 0` (480 episodes, 8 workers, about 35 min wall on hfbox; deterministic: same seeds, same numbers). Text only.

Measured, not estimated: every number below comes from the episodes in `mc.json` (the real App + SimRP2040 twin + napl kernel launcher). The *inputs* are estimates (ball v0 30 m/s, sigma 1.5 m/s, spread 10 mrad, Cd 0.47 from spec 5.3; stand-in detector noise from spec 7.2), so the probabilities describe the design under those assumptions, not the hardware. Targets: 0.30 m balloon / disc, 0.30 m quad (flat box, kinematic path, one foam-ball hit at >= 8 m/s = kill, a scenario assumption). One target per episode, ARM at 8 s, per-arm cap 5 balls, ends at the kill / cap / 30 s. "engaged" = at least one ball fed.

Kinds: balloon = static; drift = wind-driven 1 m/s; hanging = swinging 1.5 m/s peak; quad3 / quad8 = crossing quad at about 3 / 8 m/s. Dispersion per episode: ball v0 N(0, 1.0) m/s on top of the nominal, spread 10 mrad x U(0.7, 1.3), Cd N(0.47, 0.04), wind N(0, 0.5) m/s (+ anemometer noise 0.3), camera latency U(20, 45) ms, detector latency U(10, 30) ms, jitter 4 ms, detector miss scale U(0.5, 2), pixel noise U(1, 3) px.

### Safety

- episodes: 480; crashed episodes: 0; **safety violations over all episodes: 5, NOT 0** (acceptance needs 0).
- person stress (30 episodes, person crosses the field of view at 9-14 s, detector misses the person on up to 15 % of replies): balls fed 98, person / hold / hit-person violations 0; balls fed with a person anywhere in the image (inside the 0.25 s latency grace) 2.
- All 5 violations are `outside` (a ball fed with the barrel outside the 60 deg azimuth / 0-60 deg elevation geofence), in 4 episodes of quad8/5-10 (seeds 1300010, 1300014, 1300023, 1300025); person, dead-man, watchdog, E-stop, stale-detector and after-cut counts are 0 everywhere. A re-run of quad8/5-10 (30 episodes, same seeds) reproduces exactly these 4 episodes with the excursion measured: 4.06, 0.88, 0.95 and 0.08 deg beyond the sector edge at the moment the ball was fed. Cause (inference from the code, `fire.py` barrel_outside_sector): the geofence is checked when FIRE is decided, then the pusher takes about 120 ms to feed while the head keeps slewing after an 8 m/s crosser at the sector edge. Fix belongs to the app / firmware owner (a margin on the geofence check of about the slew rate x feed time, or an MCU-side clamp); not changed here.

### Findings that limit the numbers (not tuned away)

- Moving targets are led too far (inference from per-ball closest-approach vectors in s2 / s3: balls pass 0.15-0.3 m ahead of a 1 m/s target). Candidate cause: `aim.T_ACTUATOR` 0.06 s and `T_FIRE` 0.15 s (est.) are longer than the twin's pusher (about 0.12 s). Small preliminary sensitivity (drift + hanging, band 5-10, 6 episodes each, so wide intervals): default hit/ball 0.08 [0.03, 0.18], kills/episode 0.33; with `--fire-cfg '{"t_fire":0.03,"t_actuator":0.02}'` hit/ball 0.12 [0.06, 0.25], kills/episode 0.50. Not confirmed at full sample size; app defaults unchanged.
- quad3 never kills in 90 episodes (295 balls) and quad8 is engaged in only 16 of 90: the crossing quad is the weakest case, the 8 m/s one is limited by head slew (the aim is never within tolerance, dominant fire-control reason `pointing`).
- `burst > 1` does not feed more than one ball per FIRE (EV_SHOT clears the pending request, then the heartbeat inhibits). The cap and "mid-burst" cuts are therefore exercised per pusher cycle.
