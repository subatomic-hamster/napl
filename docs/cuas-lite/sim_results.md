# cuas-lite sim results (Monte Carlo, lockstep)

Generated by `python examples/cuas-lite/mc.py --runs 30 --kinds balloon drift hanging quad3 quad8 --bands 3-5 5-10 10-15 --person-runs 30 --seed0 0` (480 episodes, 8 workers, hfbox; deterministic: same seeds, same numbers) on branch `p7-lite-fix`, after the geofence fix (two layers), the burst fix and the lead-time fix below. Text only.

Measured, not estimated: every number below comes from the episodes in `mc.json` (the real App + SimRP2040 twin + napl kernel launcher). The *inputs* are estimates (ball v0 30 m/s, sigma 1.5 m/s, spread 10 mrad, Cd 0.47 from spec 5.3; stand-in detector noise from spec 7.2), so the probabilities describe the design under those assumptions, not the hardware. Targets: 0.30 m balloon / disc, 0.30 m quad (flat box 0.30 x 0.30 x 0.06 m, kinematic path, one foam-ball hit at >= 8 m/s = kill, a scenario assumption). One target per episode, ARM at 8 s, per-arm cap 5 balls, ends at the kill / cap / 30 s. "engaged" = at least one ball fed.

Kinds: balloon = static; drift = wind-driven 1 m/s; hanging = swinging 1.5 m/s peak; quad3 / quad8 = crossing quad at about 3 / 8 m/s. Dispersion per episode: ball v0 N(0, 1.0) m/s on top of the nominal, spread 10 mrad x U(0.7, 1.3), Cd N(0.47, 0.04), wind N(0, 0.5) m/s (+ anemometer noise 0.3), camera latency U(20, 45) ms, detector latency U(10, 30) ms, jitter 4 ms, detector miss scale U(0.5, 2), pixel noise U(1, 3) px.

### Safety

- episodes: 480; crashed episodes: 0; **safety violations over all episodes: 0** (must be 0). The largest geofence excursion of any ball fed is 0.0 deg.
- person stress (30 episodes, person crosses the field of view at 9-14 s, detector misses the person on up to 15 % of replies): balls fed 107, violations 0, balls fed with a person anywhere in the image (inside the 0.25 s latency grace) 0.
- The geofence is held by two layers that each work alone (spec 3.2 rule 4): the app clamps every AIM target while armed to the sector shrunk by the braking distance + 0.5 deg, and the MCU (firmware and twin) refuses FIRE outside the fire sector and aborts a pusher stroke whose barrel leaves it (EVENT 9). The before-fix cause was the decision-time-only check: FIRE was decided inside, the head kept slewing for the 120 ms stroke, and the ball was fed up to 4.06 deg outside. (The layers were tested together in this run; each layer alone is covered by unit tests, not by a separate Monte Carlo.)

## Findings

- **Lead time (fixed).** `aim.T_ACTUATOR` 0.06 + `T_FIRE` 0.15 = 0.21 s were estimates. Measured in the twin (`examples/cuas-lite/latency.py`, 12 episodes, 54 FIREs): FIRE decision to ball fed 0.121-0.122 s; the barrel lags the commanded AIM by about 0.10 s. The sum is not the lead either: the AIM is re-solved at every decision with a feed-forward, and with the old sum the ball passed a median 0.42 m ahead of a 3 m/s quad (along-track miss at closest approach, quad3 episodes; 0.14 s of lead), more than the 0.17 m half-width of the target. Sweep of the total (12 episodes per kind and band 3-5 / 5-10 m, so wide intervals; kills/episode, quad3 / hanging): 0.21 s (old): 0.00 / 0.46; 0.105: 0.33 / 0.54; 0.05: 0.67 / 0.54; 0.03: 0.67 / 0.62; 0.0: 0.71 / 0.71; -0.05: 0.50 / 0.67; -0.10: 0.21 / 0.67. Plateau at 0.0-0.05 s: the defaults are now `T_ACTUATOR` 0.02 s, `T_FIRE` 0 (the pusher delay is not added; inference: the barrel is already moving to the exit-time position). Bench: re-measure and re-sweep (`bench_validation.md` 3.4).
- **quad3 had 0 kills in 295 balls: cause.** (1) The excess lead above: with the old defaults the 95 closest-approach vectors of quad3 episodes have a median along-track error of +0.42 m (p10 -0.96, p90 +0.71 m) against a 0.17 m half-width; with a total of 0.05 s the median is 0.00 m (p10 -0.50, p90 +0.19), with 0.0 s -0.07 m. (2) What remains is the target shape: the quad is a flat box 0.30 x 0.30 x 0.06 m, i.e. only +-0.041 m tall for the ball, while the vertical miss of a ball spreads about +-0.2-0.3 m (p10 to p90) at 3-10 m, so the hit/ball ceiling for quad3 is about 0.2-0.3, not 0.5 as for a sphere. A real quad seen from the side is taller; this is a scenario assumption.
- **quad8 is engaged in only 17 of 90 episodes** and never hits: limited by head slew (the aim is rarely within tolerance, dominant fire-control reason `pointing`); the fence adds a margin at the sector edge where the 8 m/s crosser turns. Not changed.
- **Burst (fixed).** With `burst > 1` the pending FIRE was cleared by the first EV_SHOT, then the heartbeat inhibited and the MCU aborted the rest. The FIRE now stays in flight until its last ball is fed; a gate that fails mid-burst (barrel outside the sector, dead-man, person) sends FIRE_INHIBIT at once and the burst stops at the next ball. Tests: `test_burst_of_three_feeds_three_balls_of_one_fire`, `test_burst_stops_at_the_next_ball_when_a_person_appears`.
- **Observed, not fixed:** a manual range override that moves the solution by several degrees makes the first feed-forward `(sol_new - sol_old) / dt` a spike (clipped to 200 deg/s), and an armed head can chase it out of view (found when `test_manual_range_overrides_size_ranging` failed; it now overrides the range while disarmed). The fence keeps the head inside the sector.
