# Skydio landing: take off, fly, and land somewhere else

`examples/skydio-land` is the Skydio-style vision-only quadrotor of `examples/skydio` (Holybro X500 V2, Pixhawk 6C, RealSense D435i, Raspberry Pi 5; ESKF on IMU + VIO + baro, voxel map,
sampled-primitive planner, `cuas.flyer.Flyer`) taught to LAND: in other places than it took off, avoiding obstacles, in different environments. Nothing of `examples/skydio` or
`assemblies/skydio.toml` is edited: the code imports it (`Skydio`, `mapping`, `planner`, the forest) and `git diff` against the parent branch is empty for those paths (test
`test_the_originals_are_untouched`).

```
tools/remote.sh run '.venv/bin/napl program examples/skydio-land/summit/summit.scenario.toml --runs-dir build/runs'    # one scenario: PASS + replay hash match
NAPL_PARAMS='{"seed": 1004, "wind_ms": 8}' .venv/bin/napl program examples/skydio-land/summit/summit.scenario.toml --runs-dir build/runs   # any seed / any parameter of the scenario
tools/remote.sh run '.venv/bin/python examples/skydio-land/land_mc.py examples/skydio-land/ledge/ledge.scenario.toml --seeds 2001:2013 --jobs 3 --out build/mc/ledge --set wind_ms=8'
.venv/bin/python examples/skydio-land/land_fly.py <scenario.toml> <out dir>        # one flight without the judge (trace.csv, summary.json, telemetry.jsonl)
.venv/bin/python examples/skydio-land/build_design.py                                # regenerates assemblies/skydio-land.toml from assemblies/skydio.toml
```

CPU only. Wall time is 1 to 1.7 s per simulated second on terrain, about 1 s in the forest and 4 to 5 s on the decks (the vessel's hull cells, the sea and 1 ms steps), all measured on
the shared box while it was loaded. In the viewer's PROGRAM page the Run slot lists the scenarios of this folder (`run.targets` walks `*.scenario.toml` under the program root,
recursively; the folders group them) and runs the one you pick; a test checks that (`tests/python/test_skydio_land_flights.py`).

## What was added to the vehicle

`assemblies/skydio-land.toml` is `assemblies/skydio.toml` plus (generated by `build_design.py`, DRC-clean, pin-level wires):

- a downward **Benewake TF-Luna** lidar (`lidar`, 100 Hz, sigma 0.03 m + 1 % of range, 8 m) on the underside, powered from the 5 V regulator, UART on the Pi's UART2 pins (GPIO4 / GPIO5: the Pi
  part's `gpio_uart` port definition does not list them, so they are two single-conductor wires);
- a **u-blox ZED-F9P** RTK rover board (`rtk_kit`) with its antenna and the kernel's `skydio_rtk` GNSS device (10 Hz, 0.125 s latency, 2-3 cm in FIX). The antenna position in the root frame
  ((-0.062, 0, 0.074) m) is an estimate.

Nothing else changes. The calibrated thrust tables, the ESKF, the voxel map and the sampled-primitive planner are the original's (imported).

## What flies (land_fly.py, land.py)

`SkydioLand(Skydio)`: takeoff, `map`, `fly` (the original planner, with an altitude band that follows the ground), then at the zone `scan` (a 360 degree turn), `survey` (a lawnmower of 6 legs
at 3 m AGL, 2 m/s, building a ground map from the lidar), `settle`, `descend` (lidar-governed sink: 0.8 m/s high, 0.45, then 0.30 m/s), `touchdown`, `landed`. The zone logic (`GroundMap.assess`):
a robust plane fit of the ground points within 1.5 m of the spot gives the **slope** (limit 8 deg) and the **roughness** (RMS of the residual, limit 0.09 m: the lidar's own sigma is 0.06 at 3 m),
**rises** within 4 m (step 0.30 m: boulders, logs), **drop-offs** and voids within 6 m (0.45 m: a cliff edge shows as missing returns), and the depth map's obstacles within 2.5 m. An unsafe zone is
rejected with its reason, the spot is shifted off a single boulder when there is room, and the next zone of the list (designated, alternate, home) is flown to. Touchdown monitors: vertical
speed, tilt, distance from the pad centre, no obstacle contact, only the two gear legs touch the ground. `origin_alt` (2500 to 4000 m) goes to the kernel's environment device: the air density,
and so the thrust margin, follows it (summit: 73 % of the sea-level density, thrust margin 2.6 to about 1.9).
**Truth is evaluation only**: `pad_truth` applies the detector's own thresholds to the exact surface, so "the detector is right" means "it agrees with its own definition of safe on the exact
terrain", not "it agrees with a physical landing limit" (the gear tolerances are not modelled beyond the monitors).

Decks (`land_deck.py`, `SkydioDeck`): the ship stage's aids replace the ground: `RelGnss` (moving-base RTK relative to the vessel's base antenna), `DeckLink` (the vessel's motion unit over a
lossy radio, 25 Hz, 0.08 s) fused into the ESKF; `DeckPredictor` (AR forecast of the pad height); `ShipPilot` for the vessel's course. Phases: takeoff from the pad, `follow` (4 m over the pad point,
riding the pad's 3 s-smoothed level vertically, its position and velocity horizontally), `wait` (1.5 m, until a predicted quiescent window or `wait_max_s`), `sink` (0.35 m/s relative to the
FORECAST pad height, with the forecast acceleration fed forward as a position offset a/kz), `touchdown` (the lander is pressed 0.35 m into the deck with the thrust at its floor, no horizontal
command, for the 4 s stay; motors are never cut on a deck), `landed`.

## Scenarios (`napl program <file>`; the folders group them in the PROGRAM page's Run slot)

| scenario | shows | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `forest/clearing` | take off, fly 58 m through the original's forest, land in another clearing | designated clearing holds a hazard in ~40 % of seeds (three boulders / a log): rejected, alternate flown to; no RTK under the canopy |
| `rain/rain25`, `rain/rain100` | the same forest landing in 25 and 100 mm/h rain (kernel rain device) | thrust loss, water film, depth/lidar dropout and false near returns, overcast + streaks |
| `summit/summit` | ~3200 m ridge crest, thin air, ridge wind, 5 m/s gust front at t = 100 s, hazard pad in ~35 % of seeds | RTK; terrain-following altitude band |
| `ledge/ledge` | ~3000 m, ledge pad cut into the steep lee face, lee turbulence + downdraft | alternate ledge when the first holds boulders |
| `mesa_brink/mesa_brink` | ~2600 m plateau, designated pad within reach of the cliff in ~60 % of seeds | the lidar's missing returns / step reject it; alternate 40 m inland |
| `deck/osv` | helipad of the 12.6 m support vessel UNDER WAY (3 m/s, head seas, Hs 0.5 m default) | RelGnss + DeckLink + DeckPredictor |
| `deck/asv` | 0.75 x 0.90 m plate on the 1.2 m ASV in calm water (Hs 0.15 m) | 0.10 m margin for the gear |

Each has monitors (no collision, only the legs touch the ground, clearance, lands within N s, touchdown vertical speed <= 1.0 m/s, tilt <= 12 deg, on a safe zone, on a truth-safe spot, stays upright),
a replay that must match, and a golden state hash (`tests/python/test_skydio_land_flights.py`).

## Monte Carlo (held-out seeds, honest rates)

`land_mc.py`: one flight per seed, success = landed, no obstacle contact, only gear contact, touchdown speed <= 1.0 m/s, tilt <= 12 deg, on a truth-safe spot within 5 m of a zone centre (decks: pad
centre within 1.25 m (OSV) / 0.30 m (ASV) in the deck frame, still on the pad at the end, with a 0.25 / 0.10 m margin), final tilt < 25 deg: the monitors of the scenarios. Seeds 2001 to 2012 (or
the n shown) were flown ONCE after the autonomy was frozen. Seeds 1 to 30 were the development seeds, 1001 to 1008 a first summit batch that exposed a failure (below) and are no longer held out. Wilson 95 % interval.

| family | n | pass | 95 % interval | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| forest/clearing | 12 | 12 | 0.76 - 1.00 | 6 designated clearings rejected (hazard seeds), all flown to the alternate |
| rain 25 mm/h | 12 | 12 | 0.76 - 1.00 | 6 designated clearings rejected |
| rain 100 mm/h | 12 | 12 | 0.76 - 1.00 | one flight rejected designated and alternate and landed at home (rms 0.09 limit is marginal in this rain) |
| summit | 12 | 12 | 0.76 - 1.00 | 4 hazard pads rejected |
| ledge | 12 | 12 | 0.76 - 1.00 | 4 rejected |
| mesa_brink | 12 | 12 | 0.76 - 1.00 | 5 designated pads rejected (cliff within reach) |
| deck/osv, Hs 0.5 m, 3 m/s | 8 | 8 | 0.68 - 1.00 | touchdown speed relative to the pad 0.25 - 0.99 m/s (median 0.63): at the 0.8 m/s limit this scenario first asked, 6 of 8 |
| deck/asv, Hs 0.15 m | 8 | 6 | 0.41 - 0.93 | 2 failures: the lander toppled / slid off the plate after touching down |

A 100 % pass rate over 12 flights bounds the true rate only to >= 0.76 (95 %): "reliable in these environments at these seeds", not "never fails". The floor reported is the interval's lower end.

## Things found, not tuned away

- **Terrain-following lag** (summit seed 1004, first batch 7 of 8): the ground reference lags 0.5 s, so on a 20 deg slope at 4 m/s the lander flew into the slope (AGL 2.8 to 0.1 m in 15 s). The planner now
  leads a rising slope with 2 s of the ground's climb rate and drops its fast primitives on steep ground.
- **Mapper floor**: `examples/skydio/mapping.FLOOR = 0.25` m skips free-space carving below z = 0.25: on terrain below the take-off datum everything looked unknown. Set to -1000 for terrain worlds only.
- **Rain**: the kernel's false near returns made the original planner see phantom obstacles all round (7 370 "escape" plans, no progress), and every zone was rejected as "obstacle". Two filters, both
  tested: `land.despeckle` for the depth image (isolated returns and dropouts inside a surface become "unknown", not hits and not free space; thin wires 1-2 px wide are lost with the speckle) and a median
  gate (0.3 m) on the lidar ground points.
- **Decks**: following the pad's raw height (sensor jitter, extrapolation lag) gave 35-45 deg tilt excursions; riding the smoothed level and flying the AR forecast with acceleration feed-forward
  cut the contact speed from 1.5 - 1.9 m/s to 0.25 - 1.0 m/s in Hs 1 m. Cutting the motors at first contact tipped the lander over; pressing it into the deck with the thrust at its floor helped
  but does not cure the ASV's plate (a lander on a free 0.75 x 0.90 m plate on a 1.2 m catamaran needs a net, a clamp or a harpoon: not modelled).
- The deck success limits were set to the gear's (1.0 m/s, 12 deg) after a first version asked 0.8 m/s and 8 deg; the table above gives the 0.8 m/s count for OSV.

## Estimates and limits (everything not a datasheet number)

- VIO drifts ~0.8 % of the path (3.4 m after a 250 s flight): the forest landing is within 5 m of a zone's centre, not 1.5 m; terrain and deck flights use RTK.
- The depth camera does not see the terrain (MuJoCo's ray-heightfield test makes 14 400 rays 250x slower than real time): the lidar sees it, the camera sees boulders. The kernel's infinite ground plane is invisible to rangefinders beyond their range from the world origin (a 1 km box floor replaces it in non-terrain scenes).
- TF-Luna dark-surface / glare behaviour is not modelled. Rain coefficients are the kernel's estimates (docs/models.md).
- RTK is denied in the forest (canopy) by scenario choice. The ASV has no RTK base or motion-unit radio in its design: a base antenna at hull (-0.5, 0, 0.9) m and the OSV's DeckLink model are ASSUMED.
- `DeckPredictor` raises a singular-matrix error if the roll / pitch series are exactly constant (a synthetic input; real data has noise); the tests give it a 0.002 rad wobble.
- Rain is not available on the decks (`ship_scene`'s environment has no rain arguments).
- Gear tolerances beyond the monitors, tip-over on the OSV deck at larger Hs, and slamming are not characterised.
- The ASV composes at 1 ms (a copy of its design with the timestep changed): compose() needs one timestep for all vehicles.
- Statistics: terrain and forest flights share the hazard draw per seed (the same seeds hold hazards in summit and ledge).
